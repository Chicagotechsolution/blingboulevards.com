Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Bling Boulevards is currently a static, pre-launch website. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. The search box and waitlist form do not transmit data anywhere until the marketplace launches; nothing you type leaves your browser.

Hosting

Our hosting provider may log standard technical request data (IP address, user agent) to operate and secure the service.

When the marketplace opens

Vendor applications and buyer accounts will come with their own privacy terms, published before any data is collected.
