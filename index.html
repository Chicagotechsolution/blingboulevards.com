How the boulevard works

  1. Jewelers open storefronts

    Each vendor lists their own pieces, prices, and turnaround times. No anonymous drop-shippers — every shop is a named, verified jeweler.

  2. You buy from the maker

    Orders and custom requests go straight to the jeweler's bench. Questions about karat, stones, or sizing are answered by the person who does the work.

  3. We hold the standard

    Metal purity and stone descriptions must match documentation. A shop that misdescribes a piece loses its spot on the boulevard.
